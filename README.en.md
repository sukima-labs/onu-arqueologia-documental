# ATLAS ONU — Documentary archaeology of the United Nations

*[Leer en español](README.md)*

ATLAS ONU is an independent academic platform for researching the UN Special Procedures and the wider human rights system, and for recovering the original documents **checked against the official source**.

- **Who it is for:** students, researchers and organisations that need to find and cite UN human rights documents (mandates, treaties and treaty bodies, countries, history, acronyms) without getting lost in UN cataloguing.
- **What it does:** every current mandate from the OHCHR index, with the resolutions that created and renewed it, each verified one by one against the UN Digital Library; a profile for each of the 196 countries (ratification of the 18 core human rights instruments, visits, standing invitations, concluding observations and UPR); a world map; and full-text search inside the official documents, with the exact paragraph and a link to the official PDF.
- **Recovers documents** even when the symbol is misspelled, maps an `L.` draft to the adopted resolution, and says plainly whether a symbol is a resolution, a report or a draft.
- **Updates itself** twice a day from the official sources, and only publishes when the data does not get worse.
- **Six languages** (the UN official ones); the interface translations are machine-made and still awaiting native review. Accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA, measured), installable and usable offline.
